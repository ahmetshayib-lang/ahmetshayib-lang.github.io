## About the booklet

*A Hand in the Story* (يدٌ في الحكاية) is the digital booklet for adults within the **What We Carry** initiative. It is written for adult Syrians of any region or background, inside the country or abroad.

It doesn't teach readers that they love their country; it asks them good questions about it. It brings together stories, questions that turn an idea around, a range of voices, moments of personal reflection, and prompts for group discussion.

## The cover

An open palm drawn in one continuous line, with Syria's rivers running through its lines. The same thread runs through every page of the booklet as its visual system.
