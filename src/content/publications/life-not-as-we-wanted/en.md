## About the book

A long-form intellectual and literary book about people whose lives did not turn out as hoped, and people who keep comparing a good life with an imagined one. It brings together psychological, social, philosophical and balanced religious perspectives, with stories and analysis.

The book is being written and has not been published. The English title is a working translation.
