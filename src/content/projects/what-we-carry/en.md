## What do we carry?

Every Syrian carries something of their country: a memory, a dialect, the name of a city, a question still waiting for an answer. *What We Carry* is a cultural and humanitarian initiative that starts from that simple question, and tries to open a calm conversation about Syrian identity, belonging, collective memory, and the relationship between Syrians and their homeland — inside the country and abroad.

It is not a single book but a project that speaks to different generations in different forms:

- **A children's story**, *سرّ الخريطة التي لا تكتمل* (working English title: *The Secret of the Map That Is Never Complete*).
- **A digital booklet for adults**, *A Hand in the Story* (يدٌ في الحكاية): stories and questions about the Syria we carry, and the Syria we build.
- **Three languages:** Arabic, English and Turkish.

## The idea

The initiative does not lecture readers on loving their country; it asks good questions about it. It begins with small, specific stories and lets readers reach the meaning themselves — to see themselves as part of a larger Syrian story, with a role in what comes next.
