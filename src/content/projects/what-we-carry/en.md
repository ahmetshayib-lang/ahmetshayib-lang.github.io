## What do we carry?

Every Syrian carries something of their country: a memory, a dialect, the name of a city, a question still waiting for an answer. *What We Carry* starts from that simple question. It is a cultural and humanitarian initiative that opens a calm conversation about Syrian identity, belonging, collective memory, and the relationship between Syrians and their homeland — inside the country and abroad.

## An initiative, not a single book

*What We Carry* is an independent project that speaks to different generations in different forms. Its books are part of it, not the whole of it. So far, the initiative has produced two completed books:

- ***A Hand in the Story*** (يدٌ في الحكاية) — a book for adults: stories and questions about the Syria we carry, and the Syria we build.
- ***The Secret of the Incomplete Map*** (سرّ الخريطة التي لا تكتمل) — a story for children.

The initiative also reaches into digital space through its campaign, **Syria from the Heart**, which carries the same questions to a wider audience.

## The idea

The initiative does not lecture anyone on loving their country; it asks good questions about it. It begins with small, specific stories and lets readers reach the meaning themselves — to see themselves as part of a larger Syrian story, with a role in what comes next.
