## The same reality, through different eyes

*From Another Angle* explores how people perceive reality, how they interpret the situations they live through, why they compare their actual lives with alternatives they imagine — and how the angle we look from shapes so much of what we experience.

It is an independent publication project under the **Mate & Meaning** (متّة ومعنى) imprint.

## Editorial identity

Each chapter is built from five elements:

- **An opening story** that places the reader inside a situation before any analysis.
- **Accessible but deep analysis** — never needlessly complex, never oversimplified.
- **Multiple perspectives** on the same situation.
- **Thought-provoking questions** instead of ready-made answers.
- **A reflective conclusion** that stays with the reader after the page is closed.
