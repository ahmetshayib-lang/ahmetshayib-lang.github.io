## Two questions, one book

The book follows two kinds of people:

- **Those whose lives did not turn out as they had hoped**, and who are trying to make sense of what happened.
- **Those whose lives are relatively good**, yet who keep comparing them with an imagined life that might have been.

## Approach

It reads this human experience from several angles — **psychological, social, philosophical, and a naturally balanced religious perspective** — supported by stories and accessible analysis.

## Status

This is a substantial, ongoing writing project. It has not been published.
